# Controls

BunkerVR is played mostly with your hands. The buttons are kept to a few, and a small label next to whatever your
hands can reach tells you what to do with it, until you've done it three times. To see every label again, use
**Options > VR > Accessibility > Show Learned Hints Again**.

## Oculus Touch and Meta Quest controllers

| | Left controller | Right controller |
|---|---|---|
| **Thumbstick** | Move. Click: sprint | Turn. Up: jump, or stand up from a crouch. Down: crouch. Click: flashlight |
| **Grip** | Grab, hold, take | Grab, hold, take |
| **Trigger** | Use what the hand holds, such as the lighter | Use what the hand holds, fire |
| **X / A** | X: put away what you're holding | Hold A and flick: open the revolver. Hold A with the shotgun: check its shells |
| **Y / B** | Y: the game's menu. Hold Y: recenter your view | B: the inventory |
| **Menu button** | The game's menu (pause). On SteamVR this button opens SteamVR's dashboard instead: use Y | |

The game also pauses by itself when you take the headset off or open SteamVR's dashboard or the Meta menu.

**Mark a moment for a bug report:** hold the left thumbstick in and click the right thumbstick.

## Valve Index controllers

The layout is the same by position. The left controller's **A** and **B** do what X and Y do on Touch controllers,
and a firm press on the left trackpad is the menu button.

To grab, close your fingers around the handle **and give it a light squeeze**. You keep hold for as long as your
fingers stay closed, and let go when you open them. That way a hand resting closed near an object doesn't pick it up
by accident.

## PlayStation VR2 Sense controllers

Through SteamVR the Sense controllers work as Touch controllers, so the buttons follow the Touch layout by position:
the left controller's two face buttons are X and Y, the right's are A and B.

## With your hands

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Pick something up | Reach for it and grip |
| Throw | Swing and let go |
| Open a door, drawer or hatch | Grip the handle and pull or push |
| Tear off a nailed plank | Grip it and pull. Both hands work too |
| Turn a wheel or valve | Grip it and turn your hand around it |
| Push a button | Press it with your hand |
| Climb a ladder | Grip a rung, pull yourself up, then reach for the next one with the other hand |
| Crouch | Crouch. Pulling the right thumbstick down works too |
| Draw or holster the revolver | Grip at your right hip |
| Open the revolver | Hold A and flick it sideways |
| Empty the cylinder | Tip the revolver up and give it a shake |
| Load the revolver | With your left hand, grip at your left hip to take a bullet, then push it into a chamber |
| Close the revolver | Flick it back |
| Draw or holster the shotgun | Grip over your right shoulder |
| Pump the shotgun | Grip the pump with your left hand, pull back, push forward |
| Load the shotgun | Take a shell from your left hip and push it into the port under the gun |
| Throw a grenade | Pull the pin with your left hand, then swing and let go |
| Light the lighter | Trigger |
| Light a torch, molotov or the like | Hold a flame to it |
| Light a flare | Pull its cord with your left hand |
| Heal | Pull the bandage open with your left hand, then wrap it around your left forearm three times with your right |
| Put on the gas mask | Hold it to your face. To take it off, grip it and pull it away |
| Use a key or a fuse | Push it in, then turn your wrist (a fuse needs Grip held while you twist) |
| Open a combination lock | Touch a wheel with your fingertip, then use the trigger to turn it |
| Undo a bolt | Push the wrench onto the bolt, hold Grip and crank it anticlockwise |
| Cut a chain or padlock | Hold both handles of the cutters, open the jaws around it, bring your hands together |
| Pour fuel | Open the lid with your left hand, then tip the can over the opening |
| Read a dog tag | Take it and turn it over |
| Wind the pocket watch | Pull the trigger to wind and start it, or press the crown |
| Use the flashlight | Take it from your chest, or click the right thumbstick |
| Store an item on your chest | Hold it to the right side of your chest and let go. Grip there with an empty hand to take it back |
