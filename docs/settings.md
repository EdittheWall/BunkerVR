# Settings

All of BunkerVR's options are in the game's own menu, under **Options > VR**. Each one has a short explanation in
the menu itself; this page lists them all in one place.

## Player

| Option | Choices | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Play Mode | Seated, Standing | Sets your view height and moves the holster to suit |
| Height Offset | -20 cm to +20 cm | Raises or lowers your view height in the game |
| World Scale | 80% to 120% | Your size relative to the game world. Above 100% you're larger, so the world looks smaller. Calibrating your height sets this for you |
| Recenter View | | Sit or stand comfortably, look straight ahead and press either arrow on this row. You can also hold Y at any time |

**Recalibrate** runs Player Setup again: play mode, floor, height, and where the holster, the ammunition pouch and
the flashlight sit on your body.

## Comfort

| Option | Choices | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Turn Style | Smooth, Snap | Smooth turns continuously while the right thumbstick is held. Snap turns in fixed steps, which many players find more comfortable |
| Snap Turn Angle | 30, 45, 60, 90 | How far each snap turn rotates you, in degrees |
| Smooth Turn Speed | Slow to Very Fast | How fast smooth turning rotates you |
| Movement Vignette | Off, Weak, Medium, Strong | Darkens the edges of your view while the thumbsticks move or turn you. Your own movement is never darkened |
| Head Bob | Reduced, Off | Camera motion from walking and breathing |
| Knockdown View | No Comfort, Cutscene Screen, Vignette | How knockdowns, falls and the Beast's kills are shown: through your own eyes as the game moves you, on a large screen, or through your eyes with the edges darkened |
| Knockdown Vignette | None to Strong | How far that vignette closes in |
| Screen Effects | On, Off | Blood across your view when you're hurt, and the gas mask's view while you wear it |

Cutscenes always play on a cinema screen in a dark room.

## Movement

| Option | Choices | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Movement Direction | Head, Left Controller | Move where you look, or where your left controller points so you can look around freely while moving |
| Movement Speed | Analog, Always Full | A small push of the thumbstick moves you slowly and a full push at full speed, or any push moves you at full speed |
| Jump | Off, Thumbstick Up | Push the right thumbstick up to jump |
| Physical Crouch | On, Off | Crouch in the game by crouching in real life. The right thumbstick always works too |
| Crouch Depth | Shallow, Normal, Deep | How far your head must lower before you crouch. Choose Shallow if crouching is difficult for you |
| Room-Scale Movement | On, Off | Your character follows your real steps and leaning within your play area, and your view darkens if your head moves into a wall |

## Hands

| Option | Choices | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Finger Tracking | On, Off | Empty hands follow your fingers on the controller: open hand, point, fist or thumbs up |
| Weapon Draw | Hip Holster, A Button | Grip at your right hip to draw the revolver, or press A |
| Ammo Pickup | Hip Pouch, Anywhere | Take bullets from your left hip, or by gripping anywhere away from the weapon, which is easier seated or with limited reach |
| Ammo Counter | On Ammo Pouch, On Weapon | Where your loaded and spare rounds are shown |
| Weapon Collision | On, Off | Your weapon collides with objects instead of passing through them |

## Accessibility

| Option | Choices | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Interaction Hints | Until Learned, Always, Off | The small labels that show how to use what your hands reach |
| Show Learned Hints Again | | Every hint shows again until you've used it three times |
| Bandage Wraps | 1, 2, 3 | How many times the bandage must go around your arm to heal you |
| Throw Assist | Off to High | Adds speed to thrown objects, so a gentle throw still travels far |
| Grab Reach | Short to Very Long | How far beyond your hand you can grab and use objects |
| Controller Vibration | Off to Strong | How strongly the controllers vibrate |
| Menu Pointer Smoothing | Off to Strong | Steadies the menu pointer against hand tremor |

## System

| Option | Choices | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Menu Distance | Near, Normal, Far | How far away the game's menus and messages appear |
| Menu Size | Small, Normal, Large | How large they appear |
| Spectator Smoothing | Off to Strong | For viewers and recordings: the view on your monitor follows your head smoothly and keeps the horizon level. Off mirrors the headset |
| Spectator Field of View | Narrow, Normal, Wide | How much the spectator view shows |
| Keyboard and Mouse | On, Off | Off: only the controllers work while you play in VR, so a stray key press does nothing. Ctrl+F12 switches it at any time |

Settings are saved in `BunkerVR_settings.txt` in the game's folder.
